I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Dr. Milos Hauskrecht. My research focuses on neural models for time series forecasting and temporal point processes, with an emphasis on irregularly sampled multivariate data. I also apply these methods to predict future blood pressure in intensive care patients, working closely with Dr. Gilles Clermont and Dr. Joo Heung Yoon. I am fortunate to have Dr. Milos Hauskrecht, Dr. Adriana Kovashka, Dr. Xiaowei Jia, and Dr. Geoffrey J. Gordon on my doctoral committee.

Before joining the Ph.D. program, I was a Machine Learning Scientist at Cerebellum Capital, a San Francisco-based hedge fund, where I worked closely with Dr. David Andre and Dr. Lisa Borland. Previously, I was a Research Programmer at Infocusp Innovations. I hold an M.S. in Information Science from the University of Pittsburgh and a B.E. in Information Technology from L.D. College of Engineering.
